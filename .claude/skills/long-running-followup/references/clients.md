# Other clients

For known hosts, read only their route: [Codex](codex.md) or [Claude Code](claude.md).

For Copilot and other hosts, inspect the available delegation, completion-event and scheduling
tools. Verify process lifetime, result delivery and authorization before arranging a persistent
worker. A blocking wait is not a scheduler, and a queued message is not proof of parent wakeup.
If a required capability is missing, report the gap rather than building an unverified bridge.

[Research sources](research.md) are background material, not required reading for normal use.
